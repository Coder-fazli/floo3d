# MyHomeStyler — Business Ideas & Feature Roadmap

## High Impact (Build Next)

### 1. Room Scanner via Phone Camera
User points phone at their room → AI generates a redesign in real time. No floor plan needed.
- Unique angle no competitor does simply
- Could be a standalone mobile feature

### 2. Mood Board Generator
User picks colors, furniture style, vibe → AI generates a full room concept.
- Great for homeowners who don't have a floor plan yet
- Drives top-of-funnel signups

### 3. "What Style Am I?" Quiz
5 questions → AI recommends the perfect interior style → automatically applies it.
- Drives engagement and signups
- Viral potential

### 4. Share & Get Feedback
Users share render with a public link → others can comment/react.
- Builds community
- Drives viral traffic

### 5. Compare Mode
Side by side 3 different AI styles of the same room at once.
- Users love comparison shopping for design

### 6. Renovation Cost Estimator ⭐ Most Unique
After generating render → "This redesign would cost approximately $8,000–$12,000"
- Nobody in the market does this
- Turns a design tool into a planning tool
- Completely different value proposition

### 7. Save & Iterate
Users build on previous renders — "I like this but make the walls darker."
- Chain of generations on the same project

### 8. Client Presentation Mode
Full screen slideshow of before/after with your branding.
- Real estate agents and architects would pay for this alone
- No competitor has this as a dedicated feature

## Medium Impact

### 9. Furniture Shopping Integration
After render → "Find these exact items on Amazon/IKEA" with real product links.
- Affiliate revenue on top of subscriptions

### 10. Color Palette Extractor
Upload any image (Pinterest, Instagram) → AI extracts the color palette → applies it to your room.
- Designers love this

### 11. Natural Light Simulator
"Show this room at sunrise / midday / sunset / night."
- Same design, different lighting
- Architects use this for client presentations

### 12. Virtual Staging for Empty Rooms
Upload empty room photo → AI furnishes it completely.
- Huge for real estate photographers and agents

### 13. Exterior Redesign
Upload house exterior photo → redesign facade, paint color, landscaping.
- Nobody in the niche does exterior well

### 14. AI Interior Design Chat
"Make it more cozy" "Add more plants" "Change the sofa to blue"
- Iterate through conversation instead of dropdowns

---

## B2B / Agency Features (High Revenue Potential)

### 15. White Label ⭐ High Ticket
Agencies pay to embed your tool on their own website with their branding.
- One deal = $200-500/month = 20-50 individual subscriptions

### 16. Client Portal ⭐ Most Unique in Market
Designer creates a project → shares link with client → client can browse renders and approve/reject.
- Removes back-and-forth emails
- No competitor has this

### 17. Team Workspaces
Multiple designers sharing projects under one agency account.
- Unlocks B2B pricing tier

### 18. API Access
Developers and agencies call your generation API directly.
- Build once, sell to hundreds of apps

---

## Viral / Growth Features

### 19. Daily Design Challenge
"Today's challenge: Redesign a kitchen under $5,000" — community submissions, voting.
- Free marketing

### 20. Before/After Social Card
One click generates a perfectly formatted Instagram/TikTok card with your branding.
- Every share is free advertising
- Easy to build fast

### 21. Designer Marketplace
Users sell their prompt templates/styles to other users. You take a cut.
- Creates a whole ecosystem

### 22. AR Preview
Point phone at your actual room → see the AI redesign overlaid in real time via camera.
- Next level WOW factor
